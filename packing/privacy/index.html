Privacy policy

Suitcase Symphony · by snugfit · last updated 13 November 2026

Short version: Suitcase Symphony does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game works fully offline and has no permission to use the internet.

What the game stores

To let you stop and continue at any time, the game saves your progress on your device: which levels you have unlocked, your best stars, unfinished levels, and which rules and bags have been explained to you. This data never leaves your device through the game, and it contains nothing that identifies you.

Backups

If you have turned on Android's backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the game's save file in your own Google account backup, and may copy it to a new phone when you switch. This is done by Android and Google under your backup settings and Google's privacy policy. We (the developer) never receive or have access to this data. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

What the game does not do

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no in-app purchases, and no access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings. Either removes all saved progress from your device.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
