Privacy policy

Cyberpunk Pachinko · by snugfit · last updated 15 November 2026

Short version: Cyberpunk Pachinko does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game works fully offline and has no permission to use the internet.

What the game stores

To let you stop and continue at any time, the game saves your progress on your device: your in-game currencies, the upgrades you have bought, the district you play in, game statistics (such as balls launched and time played), your settings, and the time the game was last open, which is used to work out in-game income while you were away. This data never leaves your device through the game, and it contains nothing that identifies you.

Backups

If you have turned on Android's backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the game's save file in your own Google account backup, and may copy it to a new phone when you switch. This is done by Android and Google under your backup settings and Google's privacy policy. We (the developer) never receive or have access to this data. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

What the game does not do

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no in-app purchases, and no access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. The game is not gambling: nothing is wagered, balls are free, and nothing in it can be bought with or exchanged for real money or prizes.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings. Either removes all saved progress from your device.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
